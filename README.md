# 🗒️ ClipStash - Your Simple Clipboard Manager for macOS

## ⭐ Overview
ClipStash is a lightweight clipboard manager designed for macOS. It helps you keep track of your clipboard history, search through your copied items, and export to NotebookLM. With a focus on privacy, ClipStash ensures that your data remains secure. Enjoy a seamless experience from your menu bar to enhance your productivity.

## 🔧 Features
- **Clipboard History:** View and manage your clipboard history effortlessly.
- **Search Functionality:** Quickly locate specific items from your clipboard.
- **Export to NotebookLM:** Easily transfer your clipboard data to NotebookLM for better organization.
- **Privacy-First Design:** Your clipboard data is kept private and secure.
- **Menu Bar Access:** Access ClipStash from your menu bar for convenience.

## 🎯 How to Use ClipStash
1. **Clipboard Management:** Once ClipStash is running, it will automatically start capturing items copied to your clipboard.

2. **Access History:** Click the ClipStash icon in the menu bar to view your clipboard history.

3. **Search Feature:** Type keywords in the search bar to find specific items quickly.

4. **Exporting Data:** To export clipboard items to NotebookLM, select the desired items and choose the export option.
